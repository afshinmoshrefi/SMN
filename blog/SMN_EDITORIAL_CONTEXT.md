# Private current-context editorial pass, September 6, 2026

The owner rejected the previous automated seasonal comparisons despite their
correct calculations. Empty research and a forced `SEASONAL_CONTEXT` angle made
the methodology the subject. The accepted manual JPM prototype instead starts
with a concrete business fact, its significance for shareholders and a reason
to continue reading. This change applies those principles to real generation.

## Flow

1. Keep the independently checked instrument, history window and annual baseline.
2. Validate dated current research before any paid call. Normal private seasonal
   packets default to `editorial_mode: current_context`. Empty research holds;
   intentional old history-only replays explicitly use `historical_review`.
3. Commission a useful reader question and evidence-appropriate angle from that
   current context and the fixed historical comparisons. The commissioning
   response cannot change the instrument, window, years or baseline. Its bound
   question replaces an automatically generated provisional question; the
   research adapter need not prewrite one. Recheck the lineup with
   the commissioned question before drafting, including existing coverage.
4. Plan and write using the existing bounded Astra pipeline. Business context
   leads; a historical comparison angle must earn its place. Latest dated results
   may orient the reader without being presented as breaking news.
5. Explain material historical disagreement beside the first historical claim.
   Supporting counts, dates, medians, overlap definitions and fact chrome may sit
   in expandable details. The contradiction itself cannot be hidden there.
6. Independently review the actual opening, company understanding, useful next
   question, headline delivery and source support. One correction is allowed.
   A matching reference ID or an accurate statistic cannot certify good writing.

The added commissioning call has no retry. The downstream limit is unchanged:
plan, at most one plan repair, write, review, at most one prose correction and
review. Thus a seasonal article uses at most seven model calls. A commissioning
veto or invalid response holds; no stronger historical sample is substituted.
The default article model remains `gpt-6-astra` with `low` reasoning effort.

## Research contract and trust boundary

`research.sources` retains original numeric source IDs, URL, subject symbol,
publication/access date, source text or verified factual reading notes, and
optional source usage limits. `context_facts` records source-linked facts with
an event date and role: `current_baseline`, `development`, `risk` or
`next_checkpoint`. `context_audit` identifies the subject, review time, reviewer
and retrieval scope. A latest pertinent baseline must have been verified as
such; a new article's publication date is not proof of a new underlying event.

The private defaults require research checked within seven days and a pertinent
baseline no older than 120 days. These are conservative editorial limits to
evaluate, not statistical optima. Scheduled checkpoints must be future/current,
while reported developments must already have occurred as of the packet clock.
No scheduled event is required when a useful unresolved operating question is
supported. Company research about another stock does not pass subject checks.

Source retrieval and semantic verification remain responsibilities of the
upstream research adapter and editor. Hashes detect changes; they do not prove
that a page was fetched, that a source supports an assertion or that an article
is engaging. This private entry point consumes reviewed research packets; it
does not install an autonomous daily research service. The example packets were
researched against issuer releases, presentations, calendars and SEC filings.

The deterministic selector still exposes provisional selection before
commissioning. That is eligibility to attempt a private draft, not approval of
its question or quality. Generation recalculates gates and commissions afresh;
an imported assignment cannot silently replace the live commissioning call.

## Review examples and limits

Actual Astra previews use JPM, Costco and Ford with the same historical inputs
as the independently checked second pass, and new September 6 company context.
The model receives no manual prototype paragraphs or prewritten headlines.
All calls, source/input hashes and earlier attempts are retained in the private
review artifact. Source factual reading notes are distinct from verbatim quotes.

The first JPM run still front-loaded accounting labels and methodological
qualifications; an automated pass did not establish owner acceptance. The
subsequent prompt correction explicitly addresses that defect and moves detailed
comparisons into expandable support. Review the actual generated examples, not
just test totals. These three equities are an editorial sample, not proof of
quality across every asset class or future market condition.

The final copy pass uses a dedicated, focused writing brief for current-context
articles, replacing the stacked legacy seasonal writing template on this path.
It reuses the prior source-bound commissioning decisions, then runs real planning,
writing and independent review again. JPM, Costco and Ford each passed in three
calls at this stage. No generated prose was manually edited. Deterministic guards
added afterward check that the opening is first and the first historical claim
is outside closed details; final articles are rechecked against those guards.

The current examples deliberately omit a hero while the owner judges copy and
reading flow. Text/layout review is separate from image approval. No unseen hero
is approved, and `visual_ready` remains false without the required image review.

No live checkout, scheduler, source-fetching configuration, publication path,
access rule, service or deployment is changed. Every private result remains
`publishable: false`. Calibrated probability remains shelved.
